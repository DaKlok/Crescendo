Privacy Policy

Crescendo · effective October 17, 2026 (updated)

Crescendo (“the app”) is developed by DaKlok (“we”). This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: we do not collect any data. Everything stays on your device or in your own Google Drive.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the PDF files you import, their thumbnails, your annotations, folders and app settings in the app’s private storage on your device. This data is not sent to us or any third party.

Camera

If you turn on hands-free page turning, the app uses the camera to detect head movements and facial gestures. The image is analysed in real time on your device using Google ML Kit (on-device face detection). Camera images are never saved, recorded or transmitted.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you use the tuner or record yourself practising. The tuner analyses sound in real time on your device and stores nothing. Recordings are saved only in the app on your device (and in your own backup, if you make one).

Free sheet music catalog

When you open the catalog, the app downloads a list of pieces from the app’s website (daklok.github.io). When you add a piece, the PDF is downloaded directly from the Mutopia Project (mutopiaproject.org). No personal data is sent – only standard web requests.

Google account and Google Drive

Backup to Google Drive is optional. If you connect your Google account, the app requests:

  • Your email address – only to show which account is connected.
  • Access to the app’s own hidden folder on your Google Drive (scope drive.appdata) – to upload, list, download and delete Crescendo backups. The app cannot see or access any other files on your Drive.

If you turn on sync between devices, the same backup is used to move your library between your own devices signed in to the same Google account; the backup records only a random device identifier and the device model name so that the app can tell which device made the latest changes.

Backups contain your imported PDFs, annotations, folders and settings. They are transferred directly between your device and Google Drive over an encrypted connection and are never sent to our servers (we do not operate any). You can disconnect your account in the app’s settings at any time and delete the backup data in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps.

Crescendo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, sold, shared with third parties or used to train AI models.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all data from your device. Backups stored in your Google Drive can be deleted from the app or from Google Drive. Backup ZIP files you export are stored where you choose and are under your control.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
