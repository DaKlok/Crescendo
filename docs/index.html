Crescendo

Sheet music reader for Android tablets

Crescendo is a free Android app for reading and annotating sheet music in PDF format. It is designed for large tablets such as the TCL NXTPAPER 14.

  • Library: import your PDF sheet music and organise it into folders and favourites.
  • Hands-free page turning: turn pages by turning or tilting your head, nodding, winking or smiling. The camera image is processed only on your device and is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Annotations: write on your music with a stylus or finger: pen, highlighter, shapes, eraser and lasso.
  • Backup: optionally back up your library to your own Google Drive (in a hidden app folder) or to a ZIP file.
